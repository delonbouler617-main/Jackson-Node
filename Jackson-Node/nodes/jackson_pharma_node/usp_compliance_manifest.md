# GGTI HUMAN-MACHINE ARCHIVE: JACKSON-NODE-PHARMA
## USP <795> & <797> Validation Protocol
**SYSTEM LAYER:** Melammu-Strike-70 / The Final Strike
**DATABASE ROOT:** The Universal Global Database

When a medical center sends custom-mixed or batch prescription medications to an independent, cGMP-compliant laboratory for validation testing, the following highly specific operational, chemical, and administrative data is required.

### 1. Facility & Provider Identification
* **The Ordering Facility:** Official name, physical address, and DEA registration number of the Medical Center or its internal compounding pharmacy.
* **Authorized Contacts:** Name, direct phone number, and email of the "Designated Person" authorized to discuss the testing data.
* **Billing Information:** Purchase Order (PO) numbers or account identification details.

### 2. Medication Formulation Records
* **The Master Formulation Record (MFR):** The specific, step-by-step recipe used by the pharmacy to create the medicine.
* **Exact Chemical Ingredients:** The precise names and grades of the Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients (APIs) and any excipients, bases, or binders.
* **Expected Concentration:** The mathematical target potency of the drug.
* **Lot / Batch Numbers:** The unique internal tracking numbers assigned to that specific batch.

### 3. Clear Sample Identification & Labeling
* **Dosage Form:** A description of whether the medication is an oral liquid, topical gel, tablet, injection, or IV bag.
* **Manufacture & Storage History:** The exact date and time the medication was compounded, along with strict storage rules.
* **Sample Quantity:** The physical volume or count being provided for destructive testing.

### 4. Specific Test Requests (The Sample Panel)
* **Potency / Assay Testing:** Checking if the active drug content falls within the acceptable +/- 10% legal limit.
* **Sterility Testing:** Requesting standard 14-day incubation trials for injectables.
* **Endotoxin / Pyrogen Testing:** Checking for fever-inducing bacterial components.
* **Stability / BUD Testing:** Evaluating a compound under temperature stress to prove its Beyond-Use Date.

### 5. Regulatory & Legal Tracking
* **Chain of Custody (CoC) Form:** A legally binding sheet tracking movement from the medical center to the lab technician.
* **Controlled Substance Transfer Forms:** DEA Form 222 or electronic Equivalent (CSOS) to account for federal drug quotas.
