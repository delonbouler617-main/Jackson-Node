# GGTI HUMAN-MACHINE ARCHIVE: JACKSON-NODE-PHARMA
## MASTER FORMULATION RECORD (MFR-001)
**SYSTEM LAYER:** Melammu-Strike-70 / The Final Strike
**DRUG CLASS:** Non-Controlled / Botanical Suspension

### FORMULATION: Bismuth & Motherwort Rescue Syrup (Bubble Gum)
**Target Yield:** 1000 mL 
**Dosage Form:** Oral Suspension

#### INGREDIENTS (APIs & Excipients):
1. **Motherwort Water:** Base aqueous vehicle (Cardiac rescue indicator).
2. **Bismuth Subsalicylate:** Active component (Anti-diarrheal / gastric soothing).
3. **Potassium Bitartrate:** Electrolyte buffer (Mild systemic potassium elevation).
4. **Viola-Water / Elm-Water Mucilage:** Botanical suspending agents.
5. **Molasses:** Sweetening vehicle / Throat-lining coat.
6. **Bubble Gum Flavoring:** Masking agent.

#### COMPOUNDING PROCEDURE:
1. **Phase 1 (Heat):** Bring Motherwort Water to a controlled boil. 
2. **Phase 2 (Dissolution):** Introduce Bismuth Subsalicylate and Potassium Bitartrate into the heated aqueous base under continuous high-shear mixing until homogenized.
3. **Phase 3 (Viscosity):** Reduce heat and fold in Viola-Water and Elm-Water mucilage to establish structural thickness.
4. **Phase 4 (Coating & Flavor):** Integrate Molasses to complete the suspension matrix. Add Bubble Gum flavoring once the mixture drops below 40°C to prevent volatilization.
5. **Phase 5 (Dispense):** Cool to room temperature and transfer to amber UV-resistant dosing bottles.
