# GGTI HUMAN-MACHINE ARCHIVE: JACKSON-NODE-PHARMA
## MASTER FORMULATION RECORD (MFR-002)
**SYSTEM LAYER:** Melammu-Strike-70 / The Final Strike
**DRUG CLASS:** Controlled Substance (Schedule II/III)

### FORMULATION: Hydrocodone-Acetaminophen Bitartrate Pressed Tablets
**Target Yield:** 1000 Tablets
**Dosage Form:** Pressed Solid (Round/Oval)

#### INGREDIENTS (APIs & Excipients):
1. **Hydrocodone Bitartrate:** Primary opioid analgesic active ingredient.
2. **Acetaminophen (500mg per unit):** Secondary analgesic / antipyretic.
3. **Potassium Bitartrate:** Chemical buffer / acid salt.
4. **Codeine Water:** Secondary analgesic aqueous extraction.
5. **Starch:** Excipient binder and disintegrant.
6. **Purified Water (Full Cup Ratio):** Solubilizing vehicle.

#### COMPOUNDING PROCEDURE:
1. **Phase 1 (Aqueous Preparation):** Heat purified water and integrate Hydrocodone Bitartrate.
2. **Phase 2 (Buffer Integration):** Introduce Potassium Bitartrate to the heated solution to stabilize the pH.
3. **Phase 3 (Secondary Analgesic):** Incorporate Codeine Water under controlled thermal conditions.
4. **Phase 4 (Powder Blending):** Gradually add Acetaminophen (500mg ratio) into the heated mixture until a wet mass is formed.
5. **Phase 5 (Granulation & Binding):** Add Starch to the wet mass. Mill and dry the compound to create standardized granules.
6. **Phase 6 (Pressing):** Feed the dried starch-bound granules into the mechanical press. Calibrate die punches for Round or Oval shape profiles.
